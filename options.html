Science Tokyo OTP settings

Choose the method you select on the Science Tokyo sign-in page. This extension fills and submits the OTP on isct.ex-tic.com.

Authenticator app

During app authentication setup, copy the Base32 setup key or the otpauth://totp link. The extension needs the same key as your authenticator app.

Gmail OTP email

Gmail access uses the read-only Gmail scope. Authorize the Gmail account that receives messages from noreply@ex-tic.com.
